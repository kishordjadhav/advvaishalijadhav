About Advocate Vaishali K. Jadhav

A Multi-Disciplinary Counsel & Legal Educator

Advocate Vaishali K. Jadhav is a practicing Advocate at the Pune District Court and Bombay High Court with substantial litigation experience across MACT, Civil, Revenue, Criminal, Family, Consumer, Trust, Society, Cyber Law, and Land Laws.

She holds an LL.M. in International Law, LL.B. in Land Laws, B.E. in Computer Engineering, Dual MBA in IT & Finance, and is currently pursuing her Ph.D. in Law at Savitribai Phule Pune University. Additionally, she brings 13+ years of IT Project Management experience with global MNCs including Tech Mahindra, AT&T, and Schlumberger.
